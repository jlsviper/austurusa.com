EXP-005 — Formally Verified Power-Converter Controller

Planned: a flexible digital PWM controller with an independent, small safety kernel between control logic and gate outputs. The research target is mechanized proofs for nonoverlap, bounded fault response, configuration guards, and controlled recovery, plus a low-voltage synchronous-buck demonstration.

Proposed / not yet verified · Digital proofs will not cover analog driver, clock-loss, or power-stage behavior · architecture and proof obligations →

engineering method

Begin with falsifiable requirements and failure modes. Compare architectures against prior art. Separate assumptions from guarantees. Use simulation, formal checks, physical implementation, and bench measurement where appropriate. Publish the revision and conditions behind each claim, including negative results.
