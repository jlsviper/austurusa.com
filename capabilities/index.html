Engineering capabilities

Circuit and system development from architecture through verification, prototyping, and measurement. The focus is physical electronics and deterministic embedded behavior, with computational methods used to explain and quantify the results.

Digital IC architecture & FPGA systems

Digital microarchitecture, deterministic timing engines, interface protocols, RTL/HDL development, assertions and testbenches, synthesis and physical-design flows. FIPE provides an example of an implemented ASIC-oriented protocol architecture; fabricated silicon is not yet demonstrated in the published project.

Mixed-signal PCB & real-time embedded systems

Analog sensing and conditioning, precision and high-voltage interface partitioning, schematic/PCB design, MCU integration, communication buses, instrument control, and hardware bring-up. The Geiger counter documents a completed physical instrument.

Power conversion, control & protection

Switching topology evaluation, flyback and synchronous-converter design concerns, feedback and timing constraints, gate-drive interfaces, transient/fault instrumentation, and power-rail analysis. The proposed formally verified power-controller safety kernel is a forward-looking research effort, not a verified or production-ready design.

Measurement automation & statistical characterization

Python-based instrument automation, parameter sweeps, device-to-device comparison, measurement uncertainty, population statistics, regression, residual-based anomaly detection, and report generation. ML is introduced only when it adds measurable value over simpler statistical or physical models.

Potential collaboration formats

  • Feasibility and architecture studies, including design and safety constraints.
  • Scoped FPGA, embedded-firmware, or board-level prototyping.
  • Device characterization, repeatability studies, and automated test/report pipelines.
  • Verification planning, requirements-to-test traceability, and independent technical analysis.

These are areas for discussion, not representations of certification status, turnkey production capability, or a staffed multi-disciplinary consulting organization. Specific deliverables depend on scope and facilities.
