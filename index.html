Austurusa

An independent open-idea organization for difficult problems in science and engineering.

open problems research publications experiments about

Austurusa studies problems that benefit from first-principles theory, computation, simulation, or physical experiment. Work is posted with its status made explicit; conjectures, research notes, simulations, measurements, implementations, and formal publications are not treated as interchangeable.

open problems

AP-002 — Adaptive causal worlds

Can a causal inference system recognize which families of causal assumptions remain compatible with new data before trusting its own intervention conclusions?

Status: open, active · problem statement v0.1 · causal inference / foundation models · full statement [PDF]

AP-001 — Prescribed Lyapunov dynamics in autonomous analog systems

Can a continuous-time autonomous analog system be synthesized whose largest Lyapunov exponent follows a prescribed monotone transfer law over a robust chaotic interval?

Status: open, active · problem statement v0.2 · nonlinear circuits / dynamical systems

research

RN-001 — Workload-Conditioned Health Residual Graphs for GPU Fleets

A workload-conditioned residual-graph framework for distinguishing GPU hardware deterioration from workload shifts and observability failures, with exposure-normalized health evidence and active diagnostic selection.

Status: Draft v0.1 · research note · not peer reviewed · full paper [PDF]

experiments

EXP-004 — FIPE — Flexible Interface Protocol Emulator

A programmable, timing-aware I²C / SPI / UART stimulus-and-observation engine for characterizing digital-interface margins; implemented in Hardcaml and routed in IHP 130 nm.

Status: RTL-to-GDS complete · reported 50 MHz timing closure and clean physical checks · implementation and layout · GitHub

EXP-003 — Handheld Geiger Counter

Battery-powered Geiger-Müller radiation counter integrating high-voltage generation, embedded pulse acquisition, LCD readout, custom PCB hardware, and experimental calibration against a commercial reference instrument.

Status: completed hardware experiment · 2022 · full report [PDF]

EXP-002 — Tempo Payment Intent Settler (TPIS)

A reference implementation for permissionless settlement of off-chain signed payment intents on Tempo, with replay, expiry, cancellation, wallet, and multi-relayer failure handling.

Status: reference-grade implementation · demo-validated on Tempo testnet · not production-audited

method

Problems are stated precisely enough to fail. The normal sequence is prior-art search, derivation, numerical validation, physical or computational experiment where appropriate, and publication. Negative results are retained when they eliminate a plausible architecture or sharpen the problem.
